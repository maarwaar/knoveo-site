AboutKnoveo.

What it is

Knoveo makes short films about the businesses hiding in plain sight: the company that sells your wrecked car, the card game that pays for a toy giant, the candy maker that employs your vet. 3 stories so far, each told in chapters and running 8 to 9 minutes.

How the films are made

Every frame is drawn in code. The scenes are three-dimensional models built for each story, the captions and counters are generated from the script, and the music and sound effects are synthesised. There are no cameras and no stock footage. The narration uses an AI voice, and AI tools help with research and production. The animation is illustrative: it shows how a business works, not what a particular place looks like.

How we handle facts

Figures come from company filings, regulators and named reporting, and the source is shown on screen beside each one. We name the year rather than saying "last year". Where a company is private, or a number is an estimate, we say so. Corrections are listed on the sources page.

Knoveo is not affiliated with any company it covers, and nothing here is investment advice.

Who makes it
